About the Project


KsuSerVer is a community-driven modded server established on October 10, 2025.

- The server's modpack changes from season to season, with mods selected by the community (ideas beyond just mods are also welcome).
- The main server runs on a paid host and is supported by voluntary donations.
- PvP is enabled, but players focus on building factories and impressive structures—we’re a hub for engineering.
- We use a whitelist and claim protection, so you can rest easy knowing your builds are safe.
- There are no /tpa or /sethome commands; instead, we build transport systems (railways, subways, portal networks).

We also have satellite servers—such as Classic, Vanilla, or even Bedrock.
(Links below. Naturally, the satellite servers run on Aternos, and it’s an absolute game-changer—change my mind)

Not just a server, but a community

A little about thirdBTP


- From the start (and still today), the server has been just one of the community's projects.
- Players are free to find partners simply to play together.
- We don't kick people for just chatting without playing, or for taking a break.
- We also build relationships with other communities and servers.

We welcome interesting ideas and people who are passionate about our favorite game and its mods, as well as engineering solutions and programming.

We have something resembling an infrastructure—a management system, "public amenities" (economy, transport) on the server, and a whole network of chat groups.
