TMUC - Tech Modpack Ultimate Collection

What is TMUC?

You've probably been looking for an interesting industrial modpacks, but have encountered a problem: they're either too heavy or too uniform.
Well, this collection aims to solve that problem!

I'm trying to gather almost all mods and add-ons under one project.
Of course, this is technically impossible (they're on different versions) and unnecessary (the mods would overlap each other's mechanics), so mods are divided into modpacks that are balanced for the appropriate versions and themes. The emphasis is on the mod developer's original intent, with crafting changes kept to a minimum.

Each modpack is relatively lightweight (50-80 mods), allowing you to get to know all the content in a reasonable amount of time.
On the other hand, if that's not enough, you can try another modpack of the collection, of which there are now over a dozen (which is more difficult to maintain than a single modpack, but I'm trying very hard to get it to a reasonable level).

I also try to create guides, translations, quests for these modpacks, and even my own short walkthroughs (I also have my own launcher).
I really hope my work will be useful to players.
